# AdLovers.online, Figma First Draft Brief

Paste this into Figma Make / First Draft before implementation review.

Design a light, editorial landing site for AdLovers.online, a senior Google Ads specialist serving small businesses and digital agencies in the US. The tone is calm, direct, evidence-based, and independent. Avoid agency cliches, dark SaaS dashboards, stock photos of smiling teams, noisy gradients, and aggressive sales language.

## Visual direction
- Warm off-white paper background
- Ink-like deep blue-green text
- Burnt orange accent used only for primary actions
- One editorial portrait placeholder, abstract chart visual
- Typography pairing: Space Grotesk for display, DM Sans for body
- Wide whitespace, thin warm-gray rules, asymmetrical editorial rhythm
- Mobile-first, accessible contrast, 44px touch targets

## Routes
1. `/`, for businesses: free second-opinion Google Ads audit
2. `/agencies`, for agencies: white-label fractional media buying

## Homepage sections
Hero, proof strip, audit checklist, why work directly, 3-step process, free audit offer, FAQ, contact.

## Agency route sections
Hero, capacity/margin/overhead proof strip, three-offer suite, agency economics callout, FAQ, contact.

## Copy principles
Use plain English. Keep the promise specific. Never say cheap overseas labor. Say senior execution, fractional capacity, margin expansion, direct access, and no unnecessary layers.

## Handoff
Figma should produce desktop and mobile frames, a small token page, button/input states, and named components matching the React implementation: Button, Header, Footer, SectionLabel, TrendVisual, AuditItem, OfferRow, FAQItem, ContactForm.
